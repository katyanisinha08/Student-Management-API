About Samrat Ashok Technological Institute (SATI)

Samrat Ashok Technological Institute (SATI) is a prestigious Grant-in-Aid Autonomous Engineering College established in 1960 by Maharaja Jiwajirao Education Society, located at Vidisha (M.P.).

Approved by AICTE and affiliated to RGPV, Bhopal, the institution offers various undergraduate and postgraduate programs, fostering technical excellence, professional ethics, and holistic student development.
